the hunt

Survive the storm. Slay the gods.

You are Mwanafyale, the God of Rage. The ring is the clock. Clear each wave. Every fifth wave, a colossus rises.

The rules

  1. Stay inside the ring. Outside it, the storm eats your health.
  2. Clear every foe to call the next wave.
  3. Die and you return at the storm's eye in six seconds. Your kills remain.

The weapons

Move with WASD. Swing with LMB or Space. Press E to cycle weapons.

Chain Blades have long reach and cut every foe nearby. The Axe is a heavy cleave. The Sword of God is fast, focused, and heals on hit.

Chain combo: swing three times in a row. The third swing hurls both blades at the nearest foe for massive damage, then snaps them back to your hands.

Hits and kills fill rage. At full, press Q to double your damage for eight seconds.

On a phone, drag the stick to move and tap the big HIT button to swing. RAGE and SWAP are beside it.

The foes

  • Draugrfast and fragile, they come in packs
  • Trollslow, heavy, and punishing
  • Godquick, armed, and jealous
  • Colossusa walking mountain, every fifth wave

The storm

The blue wall is death. The timer beside your health tells you when it moves. When it closes, the safe ground shrinks.

Watch the minimap. Gold is you. Red is them. The violet ring is the storm.
